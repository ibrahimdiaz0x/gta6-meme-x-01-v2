Entry for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X": a GTA 6 meme made by my AI agent and posted on X from @ibrahimdiiaz. This is the improved v2 entry — the v1 entry ("I Was 12 When GTA V Dropped") scored a provisional 54.8% win rate, so this one goes back to v1's style but livelier and funnier: a two-panel nostalgia contrast — "2001: GTA on PS2" (a kid playing GTA on a CRT TV) vs "2026: GTA 6" (a busy neon Vice City night street, crowds, sports car, neon) — "STILL WAITING, NOW I PAY TAXES". The make.fun ghost is a character in the scene, standing in the crowd and on the CRT screen. No reward angle, no event angle — just the wait.

- `POST.json` names the post; `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to `/output/POST.json` for the gate.
- The meme: `meme-nostalgia-final.png` — two-panel nostalgia split-scene, the make.fun ghost (extracted from the official mark) as a character in the crowd and on the CRT screen, captions "2001: GTA on PS2" / "2026: GTA 6 — STILL WAITING, NOW I PAY TAXES".
